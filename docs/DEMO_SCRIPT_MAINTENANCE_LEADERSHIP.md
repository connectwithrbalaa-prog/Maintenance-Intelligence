# Demo Script: Maintenance Leadership Audience

## Goal
Show maintenance managers and reliability leaders measurable operational impact and governance-ready follow-through.

## Tone
- Outcome-oriented
- KPI-driven
- Risk-aware

## 12-Minute Flow

### 1. Opening (1m)
"We reduce decision latency from anomaly to action, then measure whether those decisions are accepted and completed."

### 2. RCA Decision Quality in Portal (3m)
- Show run detail.
- Highlight hypothesis, confidence, and recommended actions.

Talk track:
"This standardizes first-pass decision quality and reduces dependence on individual heroics."

### 3. Execution Traceability (3m)
- Show PM proposal approval state.
- Show handoff status and history.
- Show repair-plan record.

Talk track:
"From recommendation to execution, we preserve a clean audit trail and accountability path."

### 4. Portfolio Prioritization (2m)
- Show prioritized assets.

Talk track:
"This allows supervisors to allocate labor against highest-risk and highest-impact maintenance opportunities."

### 5. Outcomes and Trends (2m)
- Show outcomes summary in portal.
- Optional Grafana pivot for leadership evidence.

Talk track:
"We track acceptance trends, handoff pressure, and follow-through quality so improvement is measurable, not anecdotal."

### 6. Trust and Deployment (1m)
"Customer-visible reads and actions are authenticated and tenant-scoped in this demo path, with full production hardening sequenced as the next rollout phase."

## Leadership Questions

### "What KPI movement should we expect in a pilot?"
"Primary targets: faster triage cycle time, improved recommendation acceptance, and reduced repeat failures for the pilot asset class."

### "How quickly can this be piloted?"
"One site/org and one critical asset class can be piloted quickly once data mapping and connector handoff paths are confirmed."

### "What are the governance controls?"
"Identity, role-aware actions, tenant scope enforcement, and approval history are all in the workflow path."

## Close
"This is not just anomaly detection; it is decision quality plus execution traceability at maintenance-team scale."
