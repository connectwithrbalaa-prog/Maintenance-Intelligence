# Vertical Customization Kit

## How to Use
Pick the customer vertical and replace the placeholders in script lines so the same portal flow sounds native to their operations.

## Universal Placeholder Set
- <critical_asset>
- <failure_mode>
- <downtime_cost_signal>
- <compliance_driver>
- <sla_or_production_target>

## Vertical Packs

### Oil and Gas
- <critical_asset>: compressor train, pump skid
- <failure_mode>: seal degradation, vibration drift
- <downtime_cost_signal>: deferred production, unplanned outage
- <compliance_driver>: safety permit and audit traceability
- <sla_or_production_target>: uptime and throughput commitments

Sample line:
"For compressor-train anomalies, this helps decide quickly and preserve safety-auditable handoff details before throughput is impacted."

### Manufacturing
- <critical_asset>: CNC spindle, conveyor drive, furnace fan
- <failure_mode>: bearing wear, thermal drift
- <downtime_cost_signal>: line stoppage and OEE loss
- <compliance_driver>: quality traceability and maintenance records
- <sla_or_production_target>: schedule adherence and scrap reduction

Sample line:
"For line-critical assets, we reduce time-to-decision and improve schedule adherence by making action recommendations and handoff state explicit."

### Utilities and Energy
- <critical_asset>: transformer cooling system, feedwater pump
- <failure_mode>: thermal overload, cavitation indicators
- <downtime_cost_signal>: service interruption risk
- <compliance_driver>: regulatory reporting and auditability
- <sla_or_production_target>: reliability and restoration SLAs

Sample line:
"For reliability-critical utility assets, this supports faster triage and traceable execution aligned to service-level obligations."

### Pharma and Life Sciences
- <critical_asset>: sterile transfer pump, cleanroom AHU
- <failure_mode>: contamination risk precursor, temperature anomaly
- <downtime_cost_signal>: batch delay and validation impact
- <compliance_driver>: GxP documentation and change traceability
- <sla_or_production_target>: batch schedule integrity

Sample line:
"The workflow preserves decision and handoff traceability needed for regulated maintenance execution and review."

### Mining and Heavy Industry
- <critical_asset>: haul-truck hydraulic system, crusher drive
- <failure_mode>: lubrication failure, thermal stress
- <downtime_cost_signal>: throughput loss and safety risk
- <compliance_driver>: safety and maintenance compliance logs
- <sla_or_production_target>: availability and production plan adherence

Sample line:
"For high-load equipment, this reduces decision delays and improves follow-through visibility for availability targets."

## Quick Customization Checklist
1. Replace 5 placeholders in opener.
2. Replace 3 placeholders in value section.
3. Replace 2 placeholders in pilot ask.
4. Keep technical/auth wording unchanged unless requested.

## Pilot Ask Template by Vertical
"We recommend a pilot on <critical_asset> class at <site_or_org_scope> for 2-to-4 weeks, tracking triage latency, recommendation acceptance, and follow-through completion against <sla_or_production_target>."
