# 15-Minute Fast Demo Flow

## Objective
Deliver a complete product narrative under strict time constraints.

## Agenda (15 minutes)
1. Problem and value framing (1 min)
2. Portal run detail workflow (6 min)
3. PM follow-through and repair-plan proof (4 min)
4. Outcomes and trust controls (3 min)
5. Decision ask (1 min)

## Script

### 0:00 to 1:00
"We reduce the time from anomaly detection to approved maintenance action, with a traceable path from recommendation to follow-through."

### 1:00 to 7:00
- Open run list and go straight to a known-good run.
- Highlight hypothesis, confidence, immediate actions, PM suggestions.
- Show evidence/context references.

Talk track:
"This is the decision layer: what happened, how sure we are, and what to do next."

### 7:00 to 11:00
- Show PM proposal status and approval history.
- Show handoff state.
- Show repair-plan snapshot and parts.

Talk track:
"This is execution clarity: approved, handed off, and operationally actionable details preserved."

### 11:00 to 14:00
- Show prioritized assets.
- Show outcomes summary.
- Mention tenant-scoped reads/actions.

Talk track:
"We can prioritize effort and track whether decisions are accepted and executed, while keeping tenant boundaries enforced."

### 14:00 to 15:00
"Recommended next step is a focused pilot with one org/site and one critical asset class over 2-to-4 weeks with agreed KPI baselines."

## If you lose 2 minutes unexpectedly
- Skip signals detail.
- Skip Grafana.
- Keep run detail, follow-through, outcomes, close.
