# Demo Objection Handling Matrix

## How to Use
- Start with the short answer.
- Give one concrete proof from the portal flow.
- Offer the next step to move from demo to pilot.

## Objections

### "How is this different from alert dashboards?"
- Short answer: "Dashboards show what happened; this workflow helps decide and execute what to do next."
- Proof point: run detail + PM follow-through + repair-plan snapshot in one flow.
- Next step: run a one-asset pilot and compare triage cycle time before and after.

### "Can we trust AI recommendations?"
- Short answer: "Recommendations are presented with confidence, evidence context, and human approval controls."
- Proof point: hypothesis and evidence references plus approval/audit trail.
- Next step: define approval policy for planner or maintainer roles in pilot scope.

### "Will this replace CMMS?"
- Short answer: "No. It strengthens decision and handoff quality into your existing CMMS process."
- Proof point: proposal approval, handoff status, and lifecycle traceability.
- Next step: connector mapping for your CMMS fields in pilot onboarding.

### "How do you prevent cross-tenant data leakage?"
- Short answer: "Demo-visible reads and actions are authenticated and tenant-scoped."
- Proof point: cross-tenant access is filtered or denied on portal, signals, reports, and outcomes.
- Next step: deploy behind trusted ingress headers for production mode.

### "Is this production ready?"
- Short answer: "This demo path is secure for customer-visible workflows; full enterprise hardening is sequenced next."
- Proof point: scoped auth coverage on high-value read and action surfaces.
- Next step: execute production checklist gates with staging validation.

### "What ROI should we expect?"
- Short answer: "Expected gains are faster triage, better acceptance of recommendations, and cleaner execution follow-through."
- Proof point: prioritized assets plus outcomes trends in portal and dashboards.
- Next step: agree on 2 to 3 pilot KPIs and baseline period.

### "What happens if data quality is messy?"
- Short answer: "The workflow is resilient to partial data and still preserves core decision fields."
- Proof point: portal safe rendering and fallback behavior.
- Next step: prioritize data cleanup backlog by highest-impact fields.

### "Can this scale across plants?"
- Short answer: "Yes, with phased rollout by tenant/site and connector maturity."
- Proof point: role-aware workflow, scoped reads, and observability foundations.
- Next step: phased expansion plan: one site, one asset class, then multi-site scale.

## Escalation Rule During Live Demo
- If an objection pulls into deep implementation details, answer at policy level first.
- Offer to follow up with architecture and rollout specifics after the live walkthrough.
