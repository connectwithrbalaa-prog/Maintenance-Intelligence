# Demo Script: Plant Operations Audience

## Goal
Show operators and frontline planners that Maintenance Intelligence reduces triage time and clarifies what to do next on shift.

## Tone
- Practical
- Task-first
- Minimal buzzwords

## 10-Minute Flow

### 1. Opening (45s)
"We are going from alarm to clear maintenance action in one workflow, without hopping across five tools."

### 2. Run List to Run Detail (2m)
- Open recent runs.
- Pick a run with clear confidence and hypothesis.

Talk track:
"This gives a short explanation of what likely happened, confidence, and immediate actions for the next shift decision."

### 3. Immediate Actions and PM Suggestions (2m)
- Highlight immediate actions.
- Highlight PM suggestions.

Talk track:
"Immediate actions are for now. PM suggestions are for planned follow-through so we do not repeat the same failure."

### 4. Repair Plan Snapshot (2m)
- Show procedure steps and parts.

Talk track:
"This is maintenance-ready detail: what to do, in what order, and what parts are needed."

### 5. Approval and Handoff Visibility (2m)
- Show proposal status, approval history, handoff state.

Talk track:
"The team can see if action was approved, where it is in handoff, and who touched it. No black box."

### 6. Prioritized Assets (1m)
- Show prioritized asset list.

Talk track:
"This helps shift teams decide what to attack first when multiple assets are noisy."

## Common Questions

### "Will this replace our CMMS?"
"No. It improves decision quality and handoff into your CMMS flow."

### "Can we trust the recommendations?"
"The run detail shows reasoning, evidence references, confidence, and follow-through trail."

### "How do we avoid seeing other sites' data?"
"Demo-visible reads and actions are tenant-scoped; cross-tenant access is filtered or denied."

## Close
"The value is faster, clearer, and more consistent maintenance decisions under real shift pressure."
